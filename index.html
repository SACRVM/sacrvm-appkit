Get it

Vendoring is the primary model: copy the versioned kit/ folder into your own repo and upgrade when you choose. No install step, no lockfile, fully offline.

  1. Download the ZIP, or clone the repo.
  2. Copy kit/ into your project.
  3. Link the stylesheet and the components you use.
